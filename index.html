Privacy Policy — Chan Health

Last updated: 2026-10-26

This application (“Chan Health”) is a personal, non-commercial integration used only by the account owner (Chan) to read their own WHOOP data via the official WHOOP Developer API for personal lifestyle summaries.

Data we access

With your consent through WHOOP OAuth, the app may read: recovery (including score, HRV, resting heart rate), cycles/strain, sleep, workouts, and basic profile information.

How data is used

  • Only to generate personal daily health summaries for the account owner.
  • Not sold, not shared with third parties for marketing.
  • Not used to train machine learning models.
  • Not medical advice or a medical device.

Storage

OAuth tokens and fetched summaries are stored only on systems controlled by the account owner for this personal use. You may revoke access in your WHOOP account settings at any time.

Contact

Questions about this personal integration: contact the account owner via their WHOOP developer app contact email.
